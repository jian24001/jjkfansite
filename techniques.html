01 / Basics

Cursed Energy 101

Almost everything a sorcerer does comes from cursed energy. Here are the terms you will hear most.
Core

Cursed Energy

Power produced from negative emotions, used by sorcerers and curses alike.

Skill

Cursed Technique

A unique ability that each sorcerer or curse may inherit or develop.

Healing

Reverse Cursed Technique

Turns negative energy into positive energy, mainly used to heal.

Ultimate

Domain Expansion

Creates a pocket space where the user’s technique cannot miss.

Strike

Black Flash

A rare critical hit that lands within a split second of cursed energy contact.

Rule

Binding Vow

A self-imposed condition that boosts power in exchange for a restriction.
