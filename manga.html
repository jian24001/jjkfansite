01 / The Manga

Gege Akutami’s Series

Jujutsu Kaisen is written and illustrated by Gege Akutami and was published in Weekly Shonen Jump. It began in 2018 as the full-length continuation of the earlier prequel story, Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College.

The series finished on September 30, 2024, after 271 chapters collected in 30 volumes.

02 / Creator

Gege Akutami

The author and artist behind the series.

Born in 1992 (Heisei 4th year) in Iwate, Japan. In 2014, he made his debut with the work ‘Kamishiro Sōsa,’ which did not gain much traction. Since he enjoyed telling tall tales as a child—often blaming the family dog for food he had snuck or items he had broken—when he was actually hit by a car on his way home, no one in his family believed that an accident had truly occurred.
