01 / The Setup

Plot

Yuji Itadori is a high school student with extraordinary physical strength who spends his time in the Occult Club. When a cursed object threatens his friends, he swallows one one of Sukuna’s fingers to save them, and the King of Curses takes up residence inside him.

Sorcerers would normally have to execute a vessel like Yuji. Instead he is allowed to live under one condition: he must find and consume the rest of Sukuna’s fingers so the curse can be destroyed for good. He enrolls at Tokyo Jujutsu High. He met his new friends, Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki, as well as his eccentric teacher, Gojo Satoru.

What began as an adventure fighting cursed spirit gradually turns into a sprawling war between sorcerers, curses, and people who want to remake the world.

02 / Lore

How This World Works

the foundation that underpins every battle and every storyline.
Origin

Curses

Cursed spirits are born from the negative emotions humans leak into the world: fear, hatred, regret.

Power

Cursed Energy

The same negative energy, refined and controlled, is what sorcerers use to fight back.

Defenders

Jujutsu Sorcerers

A hidden society trains and dispatches sorcerers to exorcise curses before they harm anyone.

Technique

Cursed Technique

A technique used by Jujutsu sorcerers to eliminate curses.

Threat

CURSE SPIRIT & CURSE USER

A cursed spirit formed from the manifestation of human fear and a jujutsu sorcerer who uses his power not to protect humans.

Home

Jujutsu High

Tokyo and Kyoto schools train young sorcerers and serve as their base of operations.

Cursed energy is a power generated from negative emotions. Witches purify it to strengthen their bodies, attack, and heal. Cursed techniques are methods witches use to dispel curses. Most are inherited or innate, and each has its own rules and limitations. Advanced practitioners incorporate Reverse Cursed Techniques for healing, Binding Vows to trade limitations for power, and Domain Expansion as their ultimate move.

Technique

Limitless

Satoru Gojo. Manipulates the space between objects, paired with the Six Eyes. Gojo’s innate technique.

Technique

Ten Shadows

Megumi Fushiguro. Summons ten shikigami from shadows. Two are destroyed and more can be tamed by defeating them.

Technique

Straw Doll

Nobara Kugisaki. Uses a hammer, nails, and a doll to curse a target through Resonance.

Technique

Cursed Speech

Toge Inumaki. Words forced onto an enemy become commands, at a cost to his own throat.

Technique

Boogie Woogie

Aoi Todo. Swaps the positions of two things with a clap, a simple trick used with great skill.

Technique

Blood Manipulation

Choso. Controls blood as a weapon, from piercing shots to armor.

Technique

Shrine

Sukuna & Yuji Itadori. A technique that embodies cooking. It consists of dismantling, cleaving, and furnace processing.

03 / The Themes

What The Story Is About

Beyond the fights, the series keeps coming back to a handful of questions about people and the choices they make.
Theme

Found Family

Friends and teachers who become each other’s reason to keep fighting.

Theme

The Value Of A Life

Yuji wants to save people, but the story keeps asking what that costs and who decides.

Theme

The Price Of Power

Strength always comes with limits, sacrifices, and binding conditions.

Theme

Fear And Humanity

If curses come from human emotion, can people ever be separated from the monsters they create?

Theme

Duty vs. Choice

Sorcerers are born into obligations, and some of them push back.

Tone

Dark Humor

Grim stakes sit next to sharp comedy, which makes the losses hit harder.
