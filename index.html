01 / The Premise

SUMMARY

In Japan, people are disappearing without a trace, and various strange, unexplained events are occurring—all stemming from supernatural phenomena. It is the duty of Jujutsu Sorcerers to deal with these issues.

Meanwhile, the fingers of Ryomen Sukuna, a legendary and feared Jujutsu Sorcerer, are scattered throughout the country. A high school student named Yuji Itadori is accidentally dragged into this world after swallowing one of those fingers, and becomes Sukuna’s vessel.
